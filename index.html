Litong Gong

Senior Algorithm Expert, Alibaba Group

My research focuses on video generation and multimodal understanding.

I am a core contributor to the training and optimization of HappyHorse, a video generation model at Alibaba. My work also spans video and image generation, variational autoencoders (VAEs), diffusion models, and multimodal representation models.

I received my master’s degree from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), in 2022.
